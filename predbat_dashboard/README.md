# Home Assistant Add-on: Predbat Dashboard

A clean, easy-to-read dashboard for [Predbat](https://springfall2008.github.io/batpred/),
made of cards you choose: drag the ones you want onto the page, in the order
you want, and leave the rest out.

![Supports aarch64 Architecture][aarch64-shield]
![Supports amd64 Architecture][amd64-shield]

- Cards you can make bigger or smaller, grouped into sections you can fold.
- An energy flow drawn as your house: solar, grid, battery and the car.
- Colourful chips for the figures that matter: the battery, an energy
  flow, rates now, cost today, the next charge, the next cheap rate,
  savings, solar and more, each with a small graphic.
- Charts of the plan: the battery's charge (with charging and exporting
  marked), the rates, and solar against the house.
- Predbat's plan as a tidy table, grouped by what it's doing.
- A few controls: Predbat's mode, and force charge / export / freeze for
  the half hour now (or any slot).
- Any other Home Assistant entity as a chip.

See [DOCS.md](DOCS.md).
