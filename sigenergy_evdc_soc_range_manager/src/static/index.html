The car's battery
Car battery: usable capacity
—
Battery size: —
Usable capacity: the kWh at the charger to go from 0 to 100%, from charges and discharges that move the SoC 10% or more, so the car's own charging losses are in it. Battery size: 100% of the battery itself, between that and the kWh you get back out of it (from the round trip, taking the losses as about even each way).
Round-trip losses: last 30 days
—
From the SoC: the kWh it takes to put 1% into the car against the kWh you get back for 1% out, added up over the latest charges and discharges (short ones too, up to 60% of SoC each way, last 30 days). That's the charger and the car; the inverter's own AC ↔ DC loss is on top (see Is it paying?).
Is it paying? Last 30 days
—
Charging costs the import rate when importing, otherwise the export rate you gave up; discharging is worth the import rate it saves, or the export rate when exporting. Prices from your rate sensors (Settings), the inverter's AC ↔ DC efficiency from Settings › Statistics.
Discharge dropouts
—
Times the car stopped discharging without being told to (not a stop from here, not unplugging). A sudden rise can mean a change in the car or its software.

Power flow card

Devices off the houseFor the Overview's diagram and the dashboard card

Things in your home with their own power sensor, e.g. a car charger or a heat pump: each is drawn coming off the house, which then shows what's left.
More
Pick its power sensor (W or kW) to show it; with no sensor it isn't drawn. Pick a plugged in entity too and it's only drawn while that's on (on, home, connected, plugged in, charging, or a number above 0), e.g. your charger's "plugged in" sensor. Up to 6. Only the drawing uses these: the add-on doesn't decide anything from them.
Take them off the house
On: the house shows what's left after its devices (e.g. "4.2 kW of 12.6 kW"). Off: its whole use, the devices included.
